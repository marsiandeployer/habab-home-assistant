Checked: 2026-10-22

Home Assistant documentation says the paid cloud service is optional and Home Assistant works fully without it; the cloud adds remote access, Alexa/Google integrations and voice speech processing. The current Nabu Casa page also markets offsite backups, better media streaming and dedicated service support. The base Backup integration creates/restores local backups; the Link service offers offsite storage.

Assist voice control can run fully on home hardware. Official documentation calls cloud voice the simplest setup option and describes local processing as an available path. Therefore customers pay for convenience/remote infrastructure and integrations, not for the basic smart-home platform or all voice control.

Open Home Foundation says it is a Swiss tax-exempt non-profit funded by commercial partner fees and donations. Nabu Casa and Apollo Automation are commercial partners contractually required to contribute a majority of profit from licensed products to the foundation. The foundation page says Nabu Casa funding comes from hardware sales and Cloud subscriptions. It also says Nabu Casa is for-profit and the partnership formalized an earlier funding relationship. No public revenue, subscriber, unit-sales or margin figure was located in the reviewed primary sources.

A 2023 Nabu Casa/Home Assistant announcement states Cloud subscription revenue funded developers working on Z-Wave JS, Zigpy and related open-source work. This supports the claim that subscriptions finance both subscriber-facing cloud services and upstream ecosystem development, but gives no financial amounts.

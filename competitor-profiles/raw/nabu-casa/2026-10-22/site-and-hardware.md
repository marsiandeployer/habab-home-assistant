Nabu Casa says it was established in 2018 by Home Assistant founders/core contributors. It describes itself as independently owned, without VC/private-equity funding. Its current offer is the Link subscription and official Home Assistant hardware. It says subscriptions and purchases fund ongoing development, with the majority of profits given to the Open Home Foundation.

Current hardware overview lists Home Assistant Green, Connect ZBT-2, Connect ZWA-2 and Voice Preview Edition. Product pages show recommended MSRP: Green $199/€179, Voice Preview Edition $69/€59, ZBT-2 $49/€45, ZWA-2 $69/€59. Hardware prices are not subscriptions; USD excludes taxes, EUR includes applicable taxes. Pages say regional prices vary by local conditions and retailer.

Home Assistant software can run on user-owned Raspberry Pi, x86-64, containers and virtual machines; official hardware is optional. Green is a plug-and-play entry device. Product pages route buyers to retailers/distributors rather than positioning the hardware as mandatory. Green blog launch post described availability via a global distributor network shipping from US, EU, UK, Australia and China.

Yellow was discontinued in 2025; the VP of Commercial wrote that Green continued to have strong sales, while Yellow sales had slowed and another run no longer made sense. This is qualitative disclosure, not revenue, units or margin.
